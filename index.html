Ad Strategy

The Angles That
Will Drive Leads

Every ad runs one message. These are the six angles we'll test and scale specifically for Synergy Structures.

Angle 01

The Before/After

"See what a Synergy storage building did for this property in 48 hours." Most powerful format for visual transformation products.

Angle 02

The Free Quote

"Get a free quote on a custom storage building — delivered and installed in your area." Zero-friction lead gen with a clear offer.

Angle 03

The Problem Call-Out

"Running out of storage space? Tools everywhere? There's a better solution." Speaks directly to the frustration buyers already feel.

Angle 04

Social Proof

"1,500+ structures built. Award-winning manufacturer. Here's what our customers say." Authority + proof = trust = leads.

Angle 05

The Process Video

Behind-the-scenes of a structure being built and delivered. Shows quality, speed, and professionalism. Builds massive trust fast.

Angle 06

Seasonal Urgency

"Get your structure installed before summer/winter. Limited slots available." Time-based pressure that drives action.
